Spell design · water college

Water spells that aren't ice

Tropes grouped by what the water does: push, control the ground, act on a body, or move you. Gold marks the suggested first set.

Force: water as pressure

First set

Water Jet

A hard line that knocks the target back and leaves a puddle where it hits.

Uses: Shove arc, wall-impact damage, Water surface. GURPS has a Water Jet: low damage, big push.

Wave

A cone or wide line that pushes everyone outward and floods the ground behind it. Clears doorways, knocks people off ledges.

Uses: ConeTargeting, Shove arc, Water surface.

First set

Geyser

Erupts under a spot and throws whoever stands there straight up. They take fall damage on landing. Works on crates too.

Uses: ArcFlight pointed up, fall damage, movable props.

Control: water as terrain

First set · building now

Wall of Water

A standing wall that slows anyone wading through, deflects shots fired through it, and soaks whoever touches it.

Uses: Wall of Fire's wall-shaped surface, line targeting, Water surface left behind. Stretch: douses fire.

First set

Whirlpool

A circle that drags units toward its centre each turn and is hard to move through. The opposite of every push spell you have.

Uses: disc surface, movement cost, a per-turn pull (new).

Rain

A large area that soaks everyone, puts out fires over a few turns, and turns the ground to water. Sets up lightning.

Uses: Water surface, Electrified Water, Wet (new).

Body: water against a creature

Drowning Bubble

A globe of water around the head. The target suffocates and can't speak, so it can't cast or negotiate.

Uses: GURPS suffocation (FP loss), a status.

Dehydrate

Pulls the water out of a body. Damage that ignores armour and hits plant and water creatures harder.

Uses: damage with armour divisor, race tags.

Water Whip

A reach attack that grapples a target or yanks it toward you.

Uses: reach, Shove arc reversed.

Utility and movement

Puddle Step

Sink into one water surface and rise from another nearby. Rewards flooding the ground first.

Healing Spring

A pool that heals whoever stands in it.

Walk on / Breathe Water

Classic utility, but useless without water terrain. Skip for now.

The missing piece: Wet

There's no Wet status yet. Most water interactions go through it: wet units don't catch fire, lose Burning, and take more from lightning. It's the cheapest way to tie every water spell together, and it lays the ground for extinguishing fire later without building that now.

Wall of Water: the plan

Yes, the same approach as Wall of Fire. Wall of Fire is almost all data: a Surface with the wall shape, a 4 m height, line targeting, a status on contact, a ground surface left behind, and a scene for the look. Wall of Water swaps the values and the scene.

New beyond data: a penalty to ranged attacks whose line passes through the wall. The surface has no field for that yet.

The tsunami look: a breaking wave caught at its peak. The face leans toward the enemy side, the crest curls over with foam, spray hangs above it, and sheets of water run down the face in a loop. It holds that pose rather than crashing, so it reads as a wall. For it to actually crash and push, it would be the Wave spell.
