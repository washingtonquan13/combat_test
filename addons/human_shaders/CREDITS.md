# Human Shaders

Skin, eye and eye-wetness shaders by MatMADNESS: https://github.com/matmadness/HumanShaders
Only the repository's `HumanShaders/` folder is vendored here (downloaded 2026-11-16); its example head scans are not.

CC BY 4.0 requires credit: "Human Shaders by MatMADNESS (github.com/matmadness/HumanShaders)" must appear in the game's credits.
The shaders build on https://github.com/RustyRoboticsBV/GodotStandardLightShader.

Upstream does not state where `Resources/Eye/*.png` and `Resources/MicroDetail/*.png` come from.
