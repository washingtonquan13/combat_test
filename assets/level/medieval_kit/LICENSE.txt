materials/*.tres replace the kit's stylized materials of the same name when
tools/kits/make_pieces.gd builds pieces/ (its 4th argument). The wood trim
atlas textures/T_WoodTrim_PH_* is the kit's T_WoodTrim with its two wood bands
replaced by medieval_wood (nails patched out, rotated so grain runs along U)
and its end-grain blocks desaturated; the metal and grey bands are the kit's.
textures/T_RockTrim_PH_* is the kit's T_RockTrim with its painted edge
highlights removed and most of its tint taken out, keeping its per-stone tone,
joints and bevel normals, with rock_boulder_dry surface detail on top.
textures/T_VineLeaf_PH.png is the kit's leaf cutout recoloured per leaf in
ivy greens with rock_boulder_dry mottling.
