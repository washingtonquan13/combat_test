# Weapon models

All CC0 (public domain) unless noted. Re-fitted in Blender so the grip is the origin
(Y-up metres, blades along +Y, barrels along +Z, shield faces toward +X).

- Quaternius, Ultimate Gun Pack: pistol, rifle.
  https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-guns-pack
- Quaternius, Medieval Weapons Pack: greatsword, throwing_axe,
  longbow, wooden_shield, buckler, tower_shield.
  https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-medieval-weapons
- CreativeTrio, Crossbow: crossbow. https://poly.pizza/m/kHb0kA11oD
- longsword (no longer worn; the Iron Sword used it before the broadsword): from longsword.zip, supplied by
  the project owner; 3ds Max + Substance Painter, author path "Kuba".
  License to confirm. Scabbard dropped (its maps are in the zip).
- broadsword (worn by the Iron Sword item): generated in Tripo Studio
  (concept image, Smart Mesh, texture), not CC0. 10.7k tris, base colour map
  only; metallic and roughness are flat constants. 1.0 m long.
