Typed AP: the core

Only what can be adopted without adding much: six rules, most of which replace something that exists today, and a dock that shows them with one strip of pips.

Adopt

Two pools, whole pips

Every unit gets Movement pips and Action pips each turn. Attack pips exist only from Extra Attack. Everything is spent in whole pips, refills at the start of your own turn, and never carries over.

Replaces the single attack action (uses_attack_action / attack_action_spent_by) and the metre budget.

The free step, then thresholds

The first tenth of your Move each turn is free. After that, each pip covers a fixed distance and is spent the moment you cross into it. Distance adds up across the whole turn, so move, attack, move again never pays twice for the same pip.

pips spent = max(0, ceil((distance this turn − Move/10) / metres per pip))

Actions can borrow movement, never the reverse

An action with no pip left in its own pool can take a fixed number of Movement pips instead. Unused Action pips never buy distance.

Generalises Ability.move_cost, which already lets actions eat movement.

Move and Attack

If you spent any Movement pip this turn and you attack, before or after, that attack takes the Move and Attack penalty. Staying inside the free step never triggers it.

One check at attack time: movement pips spent > 0.

Rapid Strike on extra attacks

Every attack after your first that isn't paid from an Attack pip takes the Rapid Strike penalty. Attack pips (Extra Attack) are the only clean extra attacks.

One counter: attacks made this turn.

Spend in a fixed order

Attacks spend Attack pips first, then Action pips, then borrow movement. Other actions spend Action pips, then borrow. With only one dedicated pool this order can never strand a pip, so no solver is needed.

Leave for later

Later, once the core works

  • Aim and Evaluate build once per turn
  • Leftover movement pips fund Retreat
  • All-Out Attack and All-Out Defense toggles
  • Wait: hold a pip for a triggered action
  • Bad footing (pips cover half the distance), sprinting for FP
  • AI pricing pips beyond "move, then act"

Not adopting

  • Spell AP and other dedicated pools
  • A Reaction pool (defenses stay off AP)
  • Stagger draining AP
  • Fractional pips anywhere
  • A matching solver for spending

Still open: whether the step comes on top of the pips or out of them, and whether a faster unit gets more pips or longer ones. The mockup uses "out of" and pips equal to metres (Move 10: 1 m free, then 9 pips of 1 m).

The pip UI

No movement bar. With whole pips the strip already says how much movement is left, so a bar would say it twice. The one thing a bar told you that pips don't is how far you can go before the next pip, and that depends on where you're going, so it goes on the path in the world: a free-step segment, then a tick wherever a pip is spent. The dock only needs to show that a pip is paid for and partly used, which is one more pip state.
