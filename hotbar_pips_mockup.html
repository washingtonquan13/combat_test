Choices in this mockup

Same spot as the Move bar. The strip sits where the Move label and bar are now, over the left edge of the panel, so the dock doesn't grow. Nine movement pips, the Action pip and an Extra Attack pip fit in about 230 px.

Cost marks show the payer, not the price. The little hexes in a slot's corner are coloured by the pool that will actually pay if you use it now. Swing shows one bone hex while the Action pip is there; once that's gone the same slot shows three gold ones. If nothing can pay, the slot dims.

Nothing is written as a number. Pips are few enough to count at a glance. The tooltip names the pool and any penalty; distance within a pip belongs to the path line in the world.

Toggles stay free. The round slots have no cost marks; they change the next attack, not the budget.

Illustrative numbers: Move 10 (1 m free, then nine 1 m pips), one Action pip, an action that has no pip borrows 3 movement pips, Jump costs 2. Slot set and labels are placeholders for the real loadout.
