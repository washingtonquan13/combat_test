3 · Toggle, turn state or action

Free toggles

Ride on any attack, cost no pips, and stack. Exclusive only inside their group.

  • Hit location (group)
  • Deceptive Attack (level)
  • Dual-Weapon Attack
  • Extra Effort (FP)

Turn states

One per turn, exclusive with each other. They change your defenses until your next turn, so they lock once you act.

  • All-Out Attack: Determined, Double, Feint, Strong (pick one)
  • All-Out Defense: Increased, Double (pick one)
  • Wait (later)

Actions

Each spends a pip and can be borrowed for.

  • Attack, Feint
  • Aim, Evaluate
  • Ready, Change Posture
  • Concentrate

Derived, never chosen

Follow from what you spent.

  • Move
  • Move and Attack
  • Rapid Strike
  • Do Nothing

Conflicts

  • All-Out Attack vs All-Out Defense: exclusive.
  • All-Out Defense greys out every attack action and every attack toggle.
  • All-Out Attack is unavailable once paid movement passes half Move. While it's on, the path stops at half Move and Move and Attack doesn't apply.
  • AoA Double excludes Dual-Weapon. A third attack bought with borrowed movement still takes Rapid Strike.
  • Aim and Evaluate are lost to paid movement past the step, and Aim is also lost to any defense roll.
  • Toggles keep their state between attacks but reset at end of turn, as now. Turn states reset at the start of your next turn.

AI cost: _score_plan already prices hit locations × Deceptive levels for every attack. Crossing in four AoA variants multiplies that by five. Price the turn states as separate plans instead: AoA (Determined) crossed with hit location only; Double and Strong at the best toggle from the plain plan; AoD as one plan with no attack. The cost becomes additive, not multiplied.

5 · The step is a pip

It can be exactly one pip if movement pips are a fixed count of ten, each Move/10 long. The book step is Move/10, so the first pip is the step for every unit. That also settles the open question of more pips versus longer pips: faster units get longer pips. Borrowing three pips then costs 30% of anyone's Move.

The green first pip is spent the moment you move, costs nothing, can't be borrowed by an action, and doesn't trigger Move and Attack. Both mockups now draw it this way in place of the STEP capsule.

6 · Decisions the port leaves open

Spells after paid movement

The book's Concentrate allows only a step, so walking then casting is illegal. BG3 players expect to move and cast.

Recommend: allow it. Attack spells take Move and Attack; other spells take no penalty.

Weapon set swap

Free now (BG3). The book says Ready, and Fast-Draw makes drawing free.

Recommend: one Ready (Action pip, borrowable), or free on an automatic Fast-Draw success.

Rapid Strike on both attacks?

The book penalises both swings. Spending as you go can't reach back to the first.

Recommend: keep the port rule (the extra one only). Fixing it would need a declare-first toggle, which is the solver the core ruled out.
