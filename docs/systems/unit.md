**Autoloads owned:** none.

## Purpose
A `Unit` (CharacterBody3D) is one combatant/NPC/party body: attributes and derived stats, HP/FP, turn action budget, status effects, movement/flight execution, ability resolution, damage/downed/death, body (CharacterModel) adoption and its animation/VFX/SFX/selection presentation. `unit.gd` is a thin facade that forwards to RefCounted components built in `_ready` (unit.gd:744-770) and Node children authored in unit.tscn (unit.tscn:27-38) or added in code (`Selection`, unit.gd:760-762). It does NOT own turn order, combat start/end (Encounter/CombatManager), pathfinding (NavigationGrid), faction relations, party membership, AI decisions, or area persistence storage (AreaState/WorldManager).

## Derived / cached state
- Every stat read is `get_stat(id)`, recomputed with modifiers on each read (stats.md); the old raw getters are gone (issue 17).
- Stat modifiers on the StatInstances (stats.md) mirrors `StatModifierBehavior`s of active statuses; kept in sync only by apply/remove (status_manager.gd:83-84, 103-104).
- `abilities`, `dialogue_options` are aliased (not copied) from `definition` (unit.gd:40, 43, 46, 61).
- `radius`/`avoidance_margin`/`height`/collision shape copied from the CharacterModel at `_enter_tree` (unit.gd:657-660); shape shared, not duplicated (unit.gd:734-741).
- `is_party_leader` is computed from PartyManager (unit.gd:540-541); `is_hovered`/`is_selected` forwarded from UnitSelection (unit.gd:483-486).
- `posed_status` is the animator's cached copy of "which status pose is held" (unit.gd:529).

- **Signals out** (unit.gd:398-477): `hover_started/ended`, `selected/deselected` (relayed from UnitSelection, unit.gd:778-781); `ability_use_started`, `ability_used` → Encounter (occupancy), animator/vfx/sfx (unit_animator.gd:190, unit_vfx.gd:92); `attacked_non_hostile_unit`; `took_damage`, `healed`; `died` → Encounter turn pruning, animator, vfx (unit_animator.gd:192); `downed_changed` → Encounter._on_unit_downed_changed (encounter.gd:232, 609), DownedTracker; `downed_tally_changed` (DeathRollsBehavior._mark, UnitCombat.stabilize) → DownedTracker; `impact_triggered`; `cast_released(action)` (emitted by `AbilityAction.release()`) → UnitVfx starts the cast VFX; `status_applied/removed/ticked` (relayed from StatusManager, unit.gd:782-784) → animator/vfx/sfx; `visual_state_changed`; `movement_started/finished`; `took_off/touched_down`; `became_idle` (relayed from UnitActionState, unit.gd:777) → CombatManager end-turn deferral. `defended(unit, kind)` (UnitCombat, a miss the target dodged, parried or blocked, at the shot's impact) → UnitAnimator plays Weapon/Dodge, Parry or Block. **Held weapons:** `UnitWeapons` (`unit_weapons.gd`, a child of unit.tscn) puts the drawn set's `WeaponData.model_scene` on `CharacterModel.main_hand_grip` / `off_hand_grip`; the set last attacked with is drawn (melee first). **Motions:** callers name StringName motion keys, never clips. `CharacterModel.motions` (a `MotionSet`, `systems/unit_system/motion_set.gd`; the humanoid one is `data/motion_sets/humanoid.tres`) maps keys to AnimationSequences (`sequences`: casts, attacks, reloads, jump) and to clips (`clips`: stances, poses, armed loop, gesture). A miss falls back by dotted category (`attack.shoot.bow` → `attack.shoot` → `attack`), then to nothing, and nothing is a valid answer: a cast with no sequence releases at once, a pose or stance with no clip doesn't play. `Ability.motion` defaults to `&"cast"`; `Ability.armed_motion` picks `armed.enter|hold|exit.<kind>`; `StatusEffect.apply_motion/remove_motion/hit_reaction_motion` are the pose, its recovery and its flinch; a pose freezes on its last frame when the clip itself doesn't loop. **Stances:** `UnitWeapons.stance()` is the drawn weapon's `WeaponData.stance_motion`, the off hand's first (a shield's guard beats the sword's), only for a weapon actually in a hand; `refresh()` emits `stance_changed`, and `UnitAnimator._idle()` returns that stance on the ground instead of `idle_animation`, so a unit with a weapon out stands in its guard and comes back to it after every clip. **Body animation:** the body's AnimationPlayer holds the clips but no longer shows them. `UnitAnimator._ready` puts a `BodyAnimationTree` (body_animation_tree.gd, an AnimationTree) beside it, reached as `UnitAnimator.playback` or `BodyAnimationTree.of(body)`, and makes the player inactive. Its root is `ClipPlayback` (clip_playback.gd), a port of the 4.4 player's own playback (one current clip and a list of fading ones, the same weights to the same mixer), so clips, crossfades, method-track moments and `animation_finished` behave as before. The tree copies the player's mixer settings (a tree's own defaults are deterministic and force discrete tracks continuous) and is advanced by hand only while a clip plays, as the player stopped processing at a finished clip. Ask `playback`, not the player, what is playing (`current_animation`, `assigned_animation`, `current_animation_position`). Not ported: queue, play sections, auto-capture, an animation's `next`. **Layers:** a body whose skeleton has `Spine` gets a layered root: the BODY `ClipPlayback` into a Blend2 filtered to the upper body (Spine and every bone under it, plus the method track so a release key still fires), a second `ClipPlayback` (UPPER) on its other input. `play_upper(clip, blend)` fades the layer in, `stop_upper(blend)` out, a non-looping upper clip fades out itself (`upper_finished`); `upper_animation` is what it shows. At weight 0 the blend passes BODY through untouched. A body without `Spine` (dragon, spider) keeps the single-playback root and `has_upper()` is false. FLINCH is `BodyFlinch` (body_flinch.gd), a SkeletonModifier3D on the skeleton that adds a hit clip's rotation, relative to its first frame, on top of the pose (upper body only on a layered body), eased in 0.05 s and out 0.2 s; a modifier, not an Add2, because the tree's mixer is non-deterministic. UnitAnimator uses them for feel only, no rule changes: an armed ability's hold loop rides the upper body over a walk (`_carry_stance`, and the whole body takes it on stopping; weapon-stance idles are not carried); a hit while walking, casting or holding a stance flinches instead of playing the hit clip, so the walk and the cast's release go on (`_flinches`; standing about, posed or aloft, the hit clip plays as before); a cast turns the head to its target unit's gaze point or its aimed point (`_look_at`, released when the sequence ends, the unit dies or a scene claims it, and only if nothing else took the head since: `CharacterModel.is_gazing_at`). A whole-body `_play` lowers the upper layer over its own blend; ladders lower it. `play_cutscene_clip(name, blend, layer)` takes a `BodyAnimationTree.Layer`. **Turn hold:** UnitAnimator keeps an attack-action ability's user busy through its clip and `turn_gap` (0.35 s) after it (`_hold_busy`, `MAX_HOLD` 6 s backstop), so `became_idle`, and with it the AI's and Encounter's next turn, waits for the recovery. **Locomotion** (owner: the unit system; `locomotion_set.gd`, `locomotion_clip.gd`; step 1 of 6, data only, nothing plays it yet): a humanoid's walking set in the shape of Baldur's Gate 3's gameplay clips: idle, walk and jog loops; a jog start (the walk blends in from the idle); six walk stops (each foot at the end of a swing, on contact, passing) and two jog stops; turns in place L/R 45, 60, 90, 135, 180, 270; a turning loop each way. `LocomotionSet` (`data/locomotion/humanoid.tres`) holds one `LocomotionClip` per clip (`assets/locomotion_animations.tres`, library "Locomotion"). Every clip is baked IN PLACE: the travel and turn are taken out frame by frame and kept on the LocomotionClip as a root curve (`root_positions`, `root_yaws`; `root_at(t)`, model space, +Z ahead), for whatever moves the unit to move it by. A loop carries its `speed`; a clip off a loop carries `enters_at` (the loop time whose pose is its first frame; `LocomotionSet.next_stop` picks the stop that comes up first) and `leaves_at` (the time of the loop it hands to whose pose is its last frame); a handover is exact, root and pose. Made, root curves included, by `tools/kimodo/make_locomotion.gd` from the Kimodo takes in `tools/kimodo/takes/locomotion` (it prints each clip's travel, turn, seam and handover corrections). Planned: a script state machine `UnitLocomotion` (Idle, Turning, Starting, Moving, Stopping, TurnLoop) driving `BodyAnimationTree.play()`, not an AnimationNodeStateMachine, since its choices need the path (distance left, first leg's angle, foot phase); loops move the unit at their speed, starts, stops and turns by their root curve, turn angles fitted by scaling the root's yaw.

## Assumptions and invariants
- Definition must be assigned BEFORE `add_child`, else the body is never adopted (warns only, unit.gd:30-33; `_enter_tree` guard unit.gd:602-604).
- Combat gating is per-unit, not global: `in_combat()` = `encounter != null and encounter.is_running` (unit.gd:897-898); `_maybe_trigger_combat` checks only this unit (unit_combat.gd:450-453). Good — not a one-fight assumption.
- `is_player_controlled()` == `faction == &"player"` (unit.gd:919-920): "player side" and "party" are one faction string. `uses_downed_rule()` builds on it (unit.gd:1246-1247).
- `is_alive()` = `not has_died and (hp > 0 or uses_downed_rule())` (unit.gd:1239-1240) — death of a downed-rule unit is ONLY `has_died`, never HP.
- Death removes from `"units"` group immediately and queue_frees (possibly delayed) (unit_death.gd:71, 113-116); everything querying units must use the group, not held references.
- A grounded unit is placed from NavigationGrid's ground, idle or walking, never by move_and_slide; only a flier is moved by physics (navigation.md, "The grid owns where a body stands"). `is_on_floor()` means nothing for a ground unit.
- Status effects are shared Resources compared by identity (status_manager.gd:117-121); per-unit state lives in ActiveStatus.
- Behaviors polled live (prevents_turn, grants_flight, to-hit modifiers, on_turn_start) work from `active` alone; on_apply side effects and stat modifiers require going through `apply()` (status_manager.gd:79-85).
- **Active defenses** (committed in 82eb3dc7): in `UnitCombat.use_ability`, every to-hit success except a critical one is followed by ONE roll against the defender's `best_defense(attacker, ability)` — the highest of Dodge, Parry, Block (ties: Dodge, then Parry); a success makes it a miss (log line "dodges"/"parries"/"blocks", `result.defense`). Dodge: melee and ranged, no limit (RAW; the Martial Arts −1 per dodge was declined). Parry = 3 + floor(Basic Attack / 2) + DB, melee only, −4 per parry already made; an empty-handed parry against an attacker holding a melee weapon is a further −3. Block = 3 + floor(Basic Attack / 2) + DB, melee and ranged, once, needs a `GearItem.is_shield` item in a melee hand. Both limits count attempts and reset in `Unit.reset_turn_actions` (the defender's own turn); saved with the unit. A shield's Defense Bonus is a DB stat modifier (wooden shield DB +2, no DR) and also adds to Dodge, at defense time (`UnitCombat.dodge_target`), so Bloodied/Exhausted halving Dodge leaves DB whole (Kromm: external bonuses after halving). Only a Unit target that `can_dodge()` — alive, not Downed, no turn-preventing status (Sleep, Stun) — so point/area abilities aimed at a Vector3 are never dodged. `hit_chance(target, ability)` = P(crit) + (P(hit) − P(crit)) × (1 − P(defense)), the same `best_defense`, from `to_hit_target` (skill + outgoing + incoming + altitude) and `SuccessRoll.chances`; the AI and the hit-chance readout both use it.
- **Resistance** (committed in 82eb3dc7): `inflict_status(effect, source) -> bool` is how abilities and surfaces put a status on; `apply_status` stays for rule statuses (Downed, Flying, summon timer, crit-fail penalty). If the status names `resisted_by`, `UnitCombat.resists` rolls `SuccessRoll.roll_vs(stat + resistance_modifier)`; a success blocks it (log line "resists"). Rolled only when the source is null (a surface) or hostile; an ally's status is never resisted. Asleep/Downed units still roll. `status_land_chance` gives the odds for the readout.
- STALE-risk: `unit_death.gd:68` assumes the dying unit is in the focused area; encounters-as-instances (background worlds) broke that — see issue 4.
- Status durations tick only on the unit's own combat turn (encounter.gd:488) — "statuses are a combat thing" assumption; out-of-combat statuses are frozen (issue 12).

- Review 2026-11-15, design notes (why it is so): `UnitDeath` holds the corpse while a cinematic stand-in exists and re-arms the full delay after it leaves, because stand-ins replicate the real unit for a scene and the corpse must outlast it (322b7c6b, 5f9e3a87; U21; it names StandIn, so a generic corpse hold is the clean form). `UnitCombat` loads `reload.tres`, the default ability templates and `downed.tres` by path string, because the data resources reach back into this script and a preload would be a cycle (comments at unit_combat.gd:429-432; U17).
- Not yet documented here (review 2026-11-15): UnitAdvantages, UnitEquipment encumbrance, UnitWeapons, UnitWearables, UnitBody, weapon sets, toggles, attack rows, size wearing and the pick area. unit_combat.gd also holds the loadout model, toggles, the ST damage table and surface contact (U1).

## Lifecycle
- **Create:** `definition` setter copies template (unit.gd:21-61) → `_enter_tree` swaps/binds body once (unit.gd:601-613) → `_ready` builds components, applies `_pending_state`, definition skills, relays signals, joins `"units"` (unit.gd:744-814).
- **Load / world travel:** WorldManager reconciles a world before it enters the tree; `load_state` stashes into `_pending_state` and applies in `_ready`, or immediately if already ready (unit.gd:1495-1513). Removed persistent units are freed by WorldManager (world_manager.gd:798-801). Statuses restored via `StatusManager.load_state` (no apply hooks — issue 1).
- **Combat start:** Encounter sets `encounter`, connects `downed_changed` (encounter.gd:232). Each turn: `reset_turn_actions` then `tick_statuses` (encounter.gd:487-488).
- **Combat end:** Encounter stabilizes Downed party members (encounter.gd:322).
- **Death:** `take_damage` → `_die` → `handle_death` + `died` → `Corpse.leave` (unit_combat.gd `_die`); summon expiry via `expire` (unit_death.gd:33-43); dependents cascade-expire (unit_death.gd:110-111).
- **Corpse** (owner: `systems/unit_system/corpse.gd`, with `ragdoll_profile.gd` and `ragdoll_part.gd`; humanoid profile `data/ragdolls/humanoid.tres`): what a dead unit leaves. It takes the body (`StandIn.BODY_NAME`) out of the unit, so the body outlives the unit's `death_cleanup_delay`, and zeroes the layer and mask of every collider on it: a corpse never blocks movement and is not in the nav grid. A body whose `CharacterModel.ragdoll` is set falls as a ragdoll: one RigidBody3D per profile part (capsule or sphere, world-only mask, exceptions with every unit), ConeTwistJoint3D to the nearest ancestor part, bones posed from the parts each physics frame, a push along the unit's facing. Once at rest (or after 5 s) the rigids and joints are freed and the pose stays. A body with no profile (dragon, spider) keeps its death clip. The corpse is also the loot: `Corpse.roll_drops` (was `Remains.drop_for`) rolls the drops into a child StashComponent ("Remains of X"), offered through `get_interactions` and picked by an Area3D on `Corpse.PICK_LAYER` (1<<20) found by `GroundClickTarget._corpse_under_mouse`. Emptied, it drops its interaction and pick area and the body stays. Not saved (spawned units don't persist). If a cinematic stand-in exists, the corpse waits for it to leave. Limits: cone joints, so a knee can bend backwards; no hover outline; a loot pick box takes a left click the way a chest does.
- **New game / dialogue / cutscene:** nothing unit-specific; cinematics use `animator()`, `anchor()`, `gaze_at()` (unit.gd:685-721).
