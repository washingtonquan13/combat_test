## Purpose
Plays authored cutscenes: a stage .tscn is a timeline of beats (line, walk, spawn, face, clip, effect, sound, wait...) against a `SceneCast` of bound roles and marks, driven by one director with one cinematic camera and overlay. `CinematicCues` decides when to auto-play (area intros, combat intros). It does NOT know about Conversations (cinematic_director.gd:13) - dialogue staging asks it, not the reverse - and it does not own camera control in gameplay (CameraDirector does).

## Stand-ins and scene templates
A staged scene never moves a gameplay unit. `StandIn.raise(unit, at, facing)` (stand_in.gd) duplicates the unit's live `CharacterModel` under a top-level child of the unit named `StandIn`, hides the unit's own body, and `strike()` (or the node leaving the tree) puts the body back. The unit's transform, collision and nav occupancy are never written. `SceneTemplate` (scene_template.gd) is a .tscn of `Marker3D` marks named by cast role; `fit(cast)` puts its origin on the `anchor_role` unit, turns -Z toward the `toward_role` unit, checks every mark for floor within 0.4 m of the origin's height and standing room (trying turns of 0, +-30, +-60 degrees), and returns `{}` when the cast (radius over `widest_radius`, flying, dead) or the ground does not fit. No fit is ordinary: the scene plays on the units as they stand.
- **Animation on the copy**: the duplicate would carry the unit's `BodyAnimationTree` and share its root (its playback), so `raise` frees it (and the shared `BodyFlinch`) and gives the copy a `BodyAnimationTree` of its own on its own player, so it layers as the unit's body does. `StandIn.play(clip, blend, layer)` goes through that tree. Reading where the original's clip had got to (`_stand_idle`, `match_original`) goes through the original's tree.
- **Layers in a scene**: `CinematicStage.clip(role, name, claims, layer, blend)`; `ClipBeat` exports `layer` (BODY / UPPER / FLINCH; UPPER with no clip lowers the upper body) and `blend` (-1 = stance blend time); `WalkPathBeat.upper_animation_name` plays over the walk from the first step and comes down on arrival. A BODY clip in a scene leaves an upper clip the scene put up in place; the first claim lowers whatever gameplay had up.
- **Paired actions**: `PairedClip` (paired_clip.gd; `lead_clip`, `partner_clip`, `partner_from_lead`, `contact_time`, `length`, `kills_partner`) and `PairedActionBeat` (beats/paired_action_beat.gd). The beat owns the distance: `places()` works out both bodies' places (midpoint and line kept, or lead on `mark`), `tick` eases both there over the first 75% of the time to contact, and `_end` puts the lead on its idle (a finished one-shot scene clip otherwise restarts, because `play_cutscene_clip` makes it the base clip) and, for a kill, sets the partner's `death_animation` to the clip, calls `die()` and seeks to the end so they do not fall twice. The unit then goes through ordinary death (model freed after `death_cleanup_delay`). First pair: `assets/paired_clips/stab.tres`, clips in `assets/paired_animations.tres` (library `Paired` on placeholder_model.tscn only), generated with kimodo.cpp and baked by `tools/kimodo/make_paired_animations.gd` from `tools/kimodo/takes/`. The unit's own stay on its hidden body and come back on `strike`.

- **Held raiders**: units that must not exist until a flag are instantiated with the area, readied, then pulled out of the tree and kept in an array on the area script (`_held_raiders`, goblin_quarry.gd:54-57, 154-162, 287-291), so nothing sees, hears or saves them; chosen in 14cb4ad9 without touching detection, AI or save. - **Limits**: effects and floating text stay on the real unit; on a stage without `performs_on_stand_ins`, clip and walk beats animate the unit itself; the copy is taken once at raise, so gear changed mid-scene does not show.

## Cutscenes keyed by hand (the minimal path)
A second kind of scene the director plays, alongside CinematicStage. Both extend `DirectedScene`
(`directed_scene.gd`), the small surface the director drives: bind, is_running, is_waiting, advance,
skip_to_end, commit_stand_ins, unbind.

- **Owner of the performance: the scene's own Timeline.** A `Cutscene` (`cutscene.gd`) has no beats.
  Position keys move a `CutsceneRole`, Animation Playback keys play its body's clips, a discrete key
  on `Cutscene.shot` cuts the camera, a Call Method key on `say(who, text)` is a line.
- **Owner of who is in it: `CutsceneRole`** (`cutscene_role.gd`). Finds its unit (caller's cast, the
  leader, or a unit of its definition; otherwise spawns one, which stays), swaps its preview body for
  a copy of the unit's (`StandIn.copy_body`), hides the unit, and puts it where the body ended.
- **Owner of where it plays: `Cutscene.anchor`** (a role whose unit already stands in the world) or
  `anchor_mark`.
- **A Cutscene can sit in an area** (owner: `CinematicDirector.play_placed(node)`). The placed node
  is out of sight in game and is never the one that acts: it keeps itself as authored
  (`Cutscene._authored`, taken on entering the tree, before its bodies add their sockets) and each
  play runs a copy beside it (`acting_copy()`), freed at the end. `scene_started`/`scene_finished`
  carry an empty PackedScene for it, so a placed scene has no authored music. K in a debug build plays
  the first one in the loaded area.
- **Clips made for one scene** live in `assets/cutscene_animations.tres` (library `Cutscene` on the
  character models; owner: `tools/cinematics/add_cutscene_clip.gd`, which takes one clip from an
  FBX imported onto the humanoid skeleton). Every clip there is in place: the hips' forward travel
  is taken out, and a clip that falls by itself has that height taken out too (`drop=`), so the
  role's position keys are the only thing that moves the body. `range=` keeps one part of a take and
  `pose=` makes a looping hold of one moment, so a jump is three clips keyed in a row and its airtime
  belongs to the role's keys, not the clip: `Cutscene/jump_takeoff` (0.9 s, perched; the feet leave
  at 0.45), `jump_fall` (a looping hold of the mid-air pose, for as long as the timeline leaves it),
  `jump_land` (1.6 s; the feet touch 0.4 s in, so it is keyed 0.4 s before the landing). `jump_down`
  is the same take whole. The arc of a jump is keyed, not coded: the role's `position:z` (and `x`)
  as plain two-key value tracks, `position:y` as a Bezier track whose handles shape the fall.
- First scene: `cinematics/townsperson_meeting.tscn` (L in a debug build). Plan and what retires:
  `systems/cinematic_system/SIMPLIFICATION_PLAN.md`.

## Shots
- `FramingRig` (framing_rig.gd) mirrors every CameraFraming field, including `pan_degrees` and `focus_blur`, through `_fill`. Keying the stage's `shot` is a cut; keying a rig's numbers is a move.
- `CameraPathRig` (camera_path_rig.gd) extends it: the eye is `path` (a Path3D, stage-owned) sampled at `progress` 0..1 by baked distance; the aim is the usual look role/mark, or `aim_path` at the same progress. Its framing (`_Riding`) never `shares_subject_with` another, so it is always a cut. No line resolves to ZERO and the camera holds.
- A held line (`wait_for_advance`) sets the timeline's speed_scale to 0, so keyed moves freeze during it.
- The goblin camp ambush opens on `camp_crane` (a ride) and ends on `hobgoblin_push` (keyed distance and fov on the unheld last bark).

## Assumptions and invariants
- One cutscene at a time; `play` refuses while running (cinematic_director.gd:151-154).
- One registered cinematic camera/overlay process-wide (cinematic_director.gd:82-95).
- Stage mounts in the focused world (spawn_parent). Marks are looked up tree-wide, first match by name (scene_cast.gd:158-180) - **STALE** with multiple resident worlds (world_manager.gd:1062): a mark of the same name in a non-focused world can win.
- Combat intro assumes the fight is in the focused world (cinematic_cues.gd:80-87) - **STALE** single-focused-world/single-fight assumption now that encounters are per-world instances.
- No save during a cutscene: can_save refuses CUTSCENE (save_manager.gd:300-302). Load is not gated by mode but world_loading cancels the scene.
- Cancel is cooperative: the polled loop checks generation each frame (cinematic_director.gd:265-327); a vanished stage cancels (:262-264).

## Lifecycle
- **Play**: mount stage under spawn_parent, bind cast, await `_run_timeline`, release claims, clear running (cinematic_director.gd:127-165). Skip/advance via `_input` (:191-199), stage `skip_to_end` (cinematic_stage.gd:510).
- **Cancel**: `_cancel` bumps generation (:200-204); camera released (:296-298); stage unbinds (cinematic_stage.gd:418).
- **World travel / load / new game**: world_loading is emitted by both `_tear_down_worlds` (world_manager.gd:344) and `_enter_world` (:678) -> cancel.
- **Combat start**: CinematicCues may play a combat intro (cinematic_cues.gd:80-87); camera released only on completion (:92-102).
- **Dialogue**: DialogueStaging plays per-node scenes when bindings exist (inert today, see dialogue.md #7) and frames speakers via `camera()`.
